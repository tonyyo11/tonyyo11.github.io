# Kandji Rebrands

Kandji, the Apple device management platform known for its elegant automation and compliance workflows, has officially become [**Iru**](https://www.iru.com/meet-iru/) (pronounced *EE-roo*).

As announced by [Weldon Dodd in the Mac Admins Slack community](https://macadmins.slack.com/archives/CG56F2SVD/p1761139711886789), this marks a defining step for the company—transforming from an Apple MDM pioneer into an **AI-powered IT security platform** that unites identity, access, endpoint management, and compliance.

For Mac Admins, this change invites both curiosity and confidence: curiosity about what “AI-powered” truly means for device management, and confidence that the same Kandji team—and their Apple-first philosophy—remain firmly in place.

# Key Changes & New Capabilities

- **New Name, Same Apple Focus**
    Apple remains the foundation. Existing Kandji configurations, devices, and agents continue operating without disruption.
    
- **Cross-Platform Expansion**
    Iru extends its reach to **Windows and Android**, allowing admins to manage mixed environments under a unified platform while maintaining Apple-first priorities.
    
- **AI-Powered Insights & Actions**
    The introduction of **Iru AI** brings context-aware automation—smart insights, self-healing actions, and predictive compliance alerts.
    
- **Integrated Identity & Access**
    Built-in passwordless authentication, passkey support, and granular admin session controls connect MDM and IAM under one platform remain a vital piece of the Iru product.
    
- **Continuous Compliance**
    AI-native compliance automation tailors custom controls, breaks them into tasks, and maps evidence automatically, keeping you audit-ready.
    
- **Seamless Upgrade Path**
    No migration headaches. Customers will be invited in cohorts for a guided in-app upgrade experience that keeps existing configurations, devices, and workflows intact.

# Impact on Mac Administration

For Apple administrators, the transition from Kandji to Iru represents **evolution, not disruption**.

Here’s what matters most for day-to-day management:

- **Stability You Can Trust**
    There is no rebuild, this is an upgrade to the current product. Your fleet keeps checking in as normal—no agent reinstalls, no new enrollment profiles.
    
- **Smarter Automation, Fewer Tickets**
    If Kandji was known for elegant abstraction, Iru doubles down—applying AI to automate maintenance, remediation, and compliance responses.
    
- **Better Cross-Team Alignment**
    Iru’s unified compliance and identity data could enable IT and Security teams to collaborate on shared dashboards and enforcement logic without leaving the Apple ecosystem.
    
---

# Implementation & Recommendations

If your organization currently uses Kandji:

1. **Wait for the Upgrade Invite** – You’ll see in-app prompts when your tenant becomes eligible.
2. **Update Admin Bookmarks** – The admin login domain is changing from *.kandji.io to *.iru.com.
3. **Verify API Integrations** – Check automation scripts, webhooks, or CI/CD connections that reference kandji.io.
4. **Review Role Scopes** – Use the new granular session controls to tighten access based on roles or temporary scopes.

## The Bigger Picture

This shift reflects a major theme across modern IT: **the convergence of MDM, IAM, and compliance** under an AI-driven architecture.

Where Kandji excelled in simplicity for Apple fleets, Iru builds on that foundation to unify endpoint and identity management across platforms—without losing the Apple-first soul that made it successful.

Their message, *“elegance through abstraction and automation as a force multiplier,”* perfectly sums up this next stage. What Kandji did for configuration and compliance, Iru aims to do for the entire security stack.

## Conclusion

Kandji’s evolution into **Iru (EE-roo)** isn’t a departure—it’s a graduation.

Admins can keep managing their Apple devices exactly as before while gradually exploring new AI and identity-driven capabilities at their own pace.

As Apple’s ecosystem becomes more declarative, intelligent, and identity-aware, Iru appears poised to grow alongside it.
